# The "Give" Templates — Sending Flyers To The Realtor

This is the lead-gen part. The flyers are the gift; **this note is how the
gift builds the relationship.** The rule: genuinely no strings. You are not
asking for anything. You made something useful, you're handing it over, done.
The business comes later, on its own, because you were the person who showed up
helpful before anyone asked.

Tone: warm, brief, peer-to-peer (per `branding/brand-guide.md`). No pitch, no
"and if you have any buyers who need financing…" tacked on the end — that
turns a give into a take and people feel it. Let the flyer's footer do the
soft intro; your note stays clean.

## After The Open House — The Natural Follow-Up (2–4 days later)

This is where a give quietly becomes a relationship. Still no pitch.

**Text/email:**
> Hey {{agent_first_name}}, how'd the open house at {{address}} go? Good turnout?

That's it. You're asking about their world. If it went well, you celebrate with
them. If a buyer needs financing, they'll bring it up — you won't have to. Log
the reply in `referral-partners/partner-tracker.csv` and set the next cadence
touch.

---

## Why No Pitch (keep this in mind)

The instinct is to add "…and if any of your buyers need a pre-approval, send
them my way!" Resist it. The flyer footer already has your name, number, and
NMLS — any buyer who's interested can reach you, and the Realtor knows exactly
what you do. Adding the ask makes the gift feel like bait and cheapens it. The
give-give-give-take model works *because* the first several touches have no
take. Trust it. The take shows up as inbound: the Realtor texts you *"can you
make me one of those for my new listing?"* — and now you're their lender.
