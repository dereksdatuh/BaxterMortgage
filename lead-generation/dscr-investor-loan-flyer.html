DSCR Loans: Qualify on the Rent,
Not Your Income

A DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan qualifies an investment property on the rent it brings in — not your personal income, tax returns, or debt-to-income ratio. It's the go-to financing for real estate investors, and it can close deals a conventional loan can't.
How it works
DSCR = Monthly Rent ÷
Monthly Payment (PITIA)
Illustrative example: $2,000 rent ÷ $1,600 payment = 1.25. A ratio of 1.00 means the rent covers the payment; higher is stronger. Some scenarios allow ratios below 1.00. (Example only — not a quote.)

You might be a fit if…

  • You're building a rental portfolio
  • You're self-employed & write-offs hide your real income
  • You've maxed out conventional financing
  • You're buying a short-term rental (Airbnb / VRBO)
  • You're purchasing in an LLC
Four flexible tiers — ask which one fits your file
Tiers differ mainly in how your rental income is counted and documented. Illustrative — features and availability are set by current program guidelines and subject to change.
Blue
Most flexible on income sources. Counts short-term rental history, and uses the lower of your lease or market rent. Factors in property-management costs.
Orange
Uses market rent for purchases. Supports long-term leased, vacant-but-rentable, and short-term rental properties.
Pink
Qualifies on rent ÷ your full payment (PITIA). A strong fit for refinances, including short-term rentals with history.
Yellow
Leans on appraised market rent. On a refinance, may use up to 120% of market rent with two months of rent receipts.
