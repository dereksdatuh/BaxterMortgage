DSCR Loans: Qualify on the Rent,
Not Your Income

A DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan qualifies an investment property on the rent it brings in — not your personal income, tax returns, or debt-to-income ratio. There are four flexible tiers, so there's usually a fit for your credit, cash flow, and down payment.
How it works
DSCR = Monthly Rent ÷
Monthly Payment (PITIA)
Illustrative example: $2,000 rent ÷ $1,600 payment = 1.25. 1.00 means the rent covers the payment; higher is stronger. (Example only — not a quote.)

You might be a fit if…

  • You're building a rental portfolio
  • Self-employed & write-offs hide your income
  • You've maxed out conventional financing
  • You're buying a short-term rental (Airbnb/VRBO)
  • You're purchasing in an LLC
