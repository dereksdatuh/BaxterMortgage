Buying a $500,000 Home: Your Options

Two of the most common paths are Conventional and FHA. Here's how they compare, and what your down payment and estimated payment could look like on a $500,000 home.

Conventional

Minimum downAs little as 3%
Typical credit~620+
Mortgage insurancePMI — cancels once you reach ~20% equity
Upfront MINone
Best forStronger credit; want to drop MI later; competitive offers

FHA

Minimum down3.5% (or 10% if credit 500–579)
Typical credit~580+ (more flexible)
Mortgage insuranceMIP — monthly; often for the life of the loan if <10% down
Upfront MI1.75% (usually financed into the loan)
Best forLower credit; higher debt ratios; first-time buyers; gift funds
