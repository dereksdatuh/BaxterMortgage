DSCR Loans: Qualify on the Rent,
Not Your Income

A DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan qualifies an investment property on the rent it brings in — not the borrower's personal income, tax returns, or debt-to-income ratio. It's the go-to financing for real estate investors, and it closes deals a conventional lender can't.
How it works
DSCR = Monthly Rent ÷
Monthly Payment (PITIA)
Illustrative example: $2,000 rent ÷ $1,600 payment = 1.25. A ratio of 1.00 means the rent covers the payment; higher is stronger. Some scenarios allow ratios below 1.00. (Example only — not a quote.)
