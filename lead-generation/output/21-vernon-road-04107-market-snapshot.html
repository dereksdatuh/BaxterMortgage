Realtor.com labels 04107 a buyer's market for June 2026 — supply is outpacing demand and homes are closing around 91% of list — yet it still ranks as a “hot” market with a 43-day median. Well-priced homes move; overpriced ones sit.

What this means for sellers

04107 is a mixed picture: it's technically a buyer's market (homes closing ~9% under asking) but still moves at a healthy 43-day median. The zip-wide median is skewed high by luxury and coastal inventory, so this home's $679K price point sits well below it — an advantage. In a market where buyers have room to negotiate, sharp pricing and showing buyers a real monthly payment (see the companion flyer) are what separate the homes that sell from the ones that linger.
