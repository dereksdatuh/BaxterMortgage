Financing a Westbrook Condo

Looking at the new condos in Westbrook (roughly $575K–$650K)? Here's how Conventional and FHA compare, what your down payment could look like, and the one thing that matters most with a new-construction condo: getting the financing lined up early.

Conventional

Minimum downAs little as 5% on a condo
Typical credit~620+
Mortgage insurancePMI — cancels at ~20% equity
Condo projectMust be warrantable (project review)
Best forMost new-construction condo buyers

FHA

Minimum down3.5%
Typical credit~580+ (more flexible)
Mortgage insuranceMIP — often life of loan if <10% down
Condo projectProject must be FHA-approved
Best forLower credit — if the project is approved
The #1 condo tip: With a condo, the lender approves the project, not just you. New-construction condos aren't always FHA/VA-approved yet, and conventional financing needs the project to be "warrantable" (owner-occupancy mix, budget/reserves, developer control, etc.). Let me check the specific building's status before you fall in love with a unit — it decides which loans are even available. And remember the monthly HOA/condo fee is on top of your loan payment.
